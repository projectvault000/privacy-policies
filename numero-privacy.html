Privacy Policy for Numero

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Numero ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. Numero is an offline-first numeracy learning app designed for learners in grades 1 to 12.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little information as possible. Depending on which features you use, we may collect:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and a general, non-personal advertising or app identifier, used to keep the app running smoothly and to serve ads (if applicable).
  • Usage data: general app activity such as which features are used (e.g. level test, practice, or test attempts) and progress data (e.g. streaks, coins, completed challenges), stored locally on your device and, where a feature requires it (such as daily challenge verification), briefly checked against our server.
  • No account or personal information is required to use the core features of the App (level test, practice, and numeracy test).

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the App's core features
  • To verify daily challenges and prevent cheating (e.g. checking device date against our server)
  • To display advertisements, where applicable, through Google AdMob
  • To understand general usage patterns so we can improve the App

3. Children's Privacy

Numero is designed to be usable by learners of all ages in grades 1 to 12, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described in this policy. Where the App is accessed by children, advertising is configured to comply with applicable child-directed treatment requirements (such as Google's Families Policy and, where applicable, COPPA). We do not require children to create accounts or provide personal details to use the App.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information beyond what is described here, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify you as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (for displaying ads, if enabled in the App)

5. Offline Functionality

Most features of Numero, including the numeracy test and practice, are designed to work offline. Data generated while offline (such as scores and progress) is stored locally on your device and may sync with our server only when a connection is available and the feature requires it (e.g. the level test or daily challenge verification).

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Any data processed on our servers (such as for daily challenge verification) is retained only as long as necessary for that feature to function.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
